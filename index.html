        How the hybrid engine works

        1. Short text (up to 2,000 characters by default) runs on Gemini Nano, on your device — private, free, and works offline once the model is downloaded.
        2. Long text goes to Gemini in the cloud, which handles long documents better.
        3. If Gemini Nano is not available in your browser, the cloud steps in automatically — you always get an answer.

        Built with Firebase AI Logic hybrid inference (on-device Gemini Nano in Chrome + cloud Gemini fallback), Firebase App Check, and Firebase Remote Config. Your text is only sent to the cloud when the cloud engine is used; history never leaves this device.
